# Research Materials

This directory contains research reference files and historical survey data:

* **Driver Behavior Survey (Responses) - Form responses 1.csv**: Sample Google Form survey responses containing driver profile details and telematics-style behavior indicators (daily mileage, harsh braking, night driving frequency).
* **google_form_fields.md**: Specifications and design details of the Google Form fields to align with the proxy dynamic pricing engine bridge in the Streamlit app.
